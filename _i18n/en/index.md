## Find Puzzles

Looking for your next challenge? Use our [Puzzle Search Tool](https://search.xivpuzzles.com) to:

- Find puzzles on your world or data center
- Filter by star difficulty, [mechanics](/subtypes), and more
- Get exact housing addresses

## Track Your Progress

Show off your achievements with our official Strange Housing card system:

- Track puzzle clears with `/clear <puzzle id>` in Discord
- Earn badges for special achievements
- Customize your card with `/setcardsettings`
- Browse available badges at [badges.xivpuzzles.com](https://badges.xivpuzzles.com)

## Join the Community

We're more than just a database - we're a thriving community of parkour enthusiasts that love building and challenging jump puzzles! We do collaborations with big venues, collaborative builds, building help, speed runs, and a lot more!

### Parkour League

Want to challenge yourself? Join the parkour league, a list of curated puzzles with different difficulty tiers. Earn a permanent role on discord by completing all of it!

### Join us for regular community events:

- Puzzle crawls (group clearing sessions)
- Clear bounties with gil prizes
- Housing design contests
- Regular raffles and giveaways
