# Strange Housing

Strange Housing aims to store all accumulated knowledge we have discovered together as a community. Here, you will be able to find in-depth information in regards to building and jumping techniques, as well as thorough explanations for the rating system and sub-types. While Strange Housing may not be the original place some of the following information originated, all of the compiled information was discovered independently by our members and the names and explanations may reflect that. We hope that this information proves useful for jump puzzle and housing enthusiasts alike, and that you feel free to share it as you like.
